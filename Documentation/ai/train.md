# Training AI models

1. Record examples for each kind of sound that you want to recognize with OpenScofo.
2. Organize them by folders. For example, inside the `tongue-ram` folder will have a lot of tongue-ram samples.
3. Download `Pd` and `py4pd`.

See also: [Audio Analysis](../descriptors/), [Advanced Configuration](../score/advanced-config/), [AI Model Reference](../descriptors/ai/).
