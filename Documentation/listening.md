# Listening Module

One of the primary objectives of `OpenScofo` is to enable the expansion of sound capabilities supported by Score Followers. As a composer, I am mainly interested in extended and percussive techniques. To make this possible, I introduced an `ONNX` machine learning module in `OpenScofo` that can be trained to be used with a variety of sounds. Here is the set of descriptors and their underlying equations used for the training process.

## Descriptors

### `Onset Detection`

Boolean value indicating whether a sound event onset has been detected. Evaluated using the `onsetsds` library's. We highly recommend the reading of the paper _Adaptive whitening for improved real-time audio onset detection_ to better understand how the onset detection works and how to choose the best method for the music you will use (Table 1 and Table 2). 

??? equation "Equations"
    The default method uses the equation 6 of the paper.

    $MKL_n = \sum_{k=0}^{K} \log \left ( 1 + \frac{|S_{n,k}|}{|S_{n-1,k}|} \right )$

    But all methods descripted in the article can be used using the [`ONSETFUNCTION`]() on config. Where S_{n,k} is a FFT bin[^1].

---

### `Root Mean Square (RMS)`

Root mean square of the amplitude representing the energy of the time-domain signal $x[n]$ of length $N$:

??? equation "Equations"

    $RMS = \sqrt{\frac{1}{N} \sum_{n=0}^{N-1} x[n]^2}$


!!! info "`OpenScofo` `RMS` descriptors match [`librosa`](https://librosa.org/) implementation with maximum differences on the order of $10^{-9}$."

---

### `dB`

Amplitude of the sound in decibels, converted from the RMS value:

??? equation "Equations"

    $$L_{dB} = 20 \log_{10}(RMS)$$

---

### `Max Amplitude `

Maximum normalized spectral amplitude detected in the current frame $X[k]$:

??? equation "Equations"

    $$MaxAmp = \max_{k} |X[k]|$$

    Where $X[k]$ is an FFT Bin[^1].

---

### `Loudness`

Perceived loudness of the sound, computed from the mean square of a filtered signal $y[n]$:


??? equation "Equations"

    $$Loudness = -0.691 + 10 \log_{10}\left( \frac{1}{N} \sum_{n=0}^{N-1} y[n]^2 \right)$$

    Where $y[n]$ is a sample without any window but filtered follow the [`ITU‑R BS.1770`](https://www.itu.int/dms_pubrec/itu-r/rec/bs/R-REC-BS.1770-5-202311-I!!PDF-E.pdf){:target="_blank"} recommendation.


**Not implemented by Librosa**

---

### `Harmonicity`

Measure of the harmonic content. Evaluated as the ratio of the peak spectral amplitude to the sum of all spectral amplitudes (skipping DC):


??? equation "Equations"

    $$Harmonicity = \frac{\max_{k>0} |X[k]|}{\sum_{k>0} |X[k]|}$$

---

### `Spectral Flatness`

Spectral Flatness is used to know the 

Indicates how noise-like a sound is. Computed as the ratio of the geometric mean to the arithmetic mean of the power spectrum $|X[k]|^2$ of length $K$:


??? equation "Equations"

    $$Flatness = \frac{\exp\left( \frac{1}{K} \sum_{k=0}^{K-1} \ln(|X[k]|^2) \right)}{\frac{1}{K} \sum_{k=0}^{K-1} |X[k]|^2}$$

!!! info "`OpenScofo` `flatness` descriptors match [`librosa`](https://librosa.org/) implementation with maximum differences on the order of $10^{-7}$."

---

### `Spectral Flux`

Measure of how quickly the power spectrum changes over time, calculated by summing the positive differences between the current and previous normalized spectra:


??? equation "Equations"

    $$Flux = \sum_{k=1}^{K-1} \max(0, |X[k]| - |X_{prev}[k]|)$$

---

### `Spectral Irregularity`

Quantifies irregularity in the amplitude of successive spectral bins:

??? equation "Equations"

    $$Irregularity = \frac{\sum_{k=1}^{K-1} (|X[k]| - |X[k-1]|)^2}{\sum_{k=0}^{K-1} |X[k]|^2}$$

---

### `Spectral Crest`

Ratio of the maximum spectral amplitude to the average spectral amplitude:

??? equation "Equations"

    $$Crest = \frac{\max_{k} |X[k]|}{\frac{1}{K} \sum_{k=0}^{K-1} |X[k]|}$$

---

### `Spectral Centroid`

Center of mass of the spectrum based on bin frequencies $f_k$:

??? equation "Equations"

    $$Centroid = \frac{\sum_{k=0}^{K-1} f_k |X[k]|}{\sum_{k=0}^{K-1} |X[k]|}$$

!!! info "`OpenScofo` `centroid` descriptors match [`librosa`](https://librosa.org/) implementation with maximum differences on the order of $10^{-5}$."

---

### `Centroid Velocity`

Rate of change of the spectral centroid over time:

??? equation "Equations"

    $$Velocity = |Centroid_t - Centroid_{t-1}|$$

---

### `Spectral Spread`

Variance of the spectral distribution around the centroid:

!!! info "`OpenScofo` `spread` descriptors match [`librosa`](https://librosa.org/) implementation with maximum differences on the order of $10^{-5}$."

??? equation "Equations"

    $$Spread = \sqrt{\frac{\sum_{k=0}^{K-1} (f_k - Centroid)^2 |X[k]|}{\sum_{k=0}^{K-1} |X[k]|}}$$

---

### `High Frequency Ratio`

Proportion of energy contained in high-frequency bands (upper 75% of bins) relative to total energy:

??? equation "Equations"

    $$HFR = \frac{\sum_{k=K/4}^{K-1} |X[k]|}{\sum_{k=0}^{K-1} |X[k]|}$$

---

### `Zero Crossing Rate`

Rate at which the signal waveform $x[n]$ crosses zero (or a threshold):

??? equation "Equations"

    $$ZCR = \frac{1}{N} \sum_{n=1}^{N-1} \mathbb{I}\{\text{sgn}(x[n]) \neq \text{sgn}(x[n-1])\}$$

!!! info "`OpenScofo` `zcr` descriptors match [`librosa`](https://librosa.org/) implementation with maximum differences on the order of $0$."

---

### `Standard Deviation`

Standard deviation of the normalized spectral power compared to the mean ($\mu = \frac{1}{K}$):

??? equation "Equations"

    $$StdDev = \sqrt{\frac{1}{K} \sum_{k=0}^{K-1} \left( |X_{norm}[k]| - \mu \right)^2}$$

---

### `Pitch` & `PitchConfidence`

Estimated fundamental frequency and confidence, calculated using the YIN algorithm's cumulative mean normalized difference function (CMNDF):


??? equation "Equations"

    $$d'_t(\tau) = \begin{cases} 1 & \text{if } \tau = 0 \\ \frac{d_t(\tau)}{\frac{1}{\tau} \sum_{j=1}^{\tau} d_t(j)} & \text{otherwise} \end{cases}$$

---

### `Power`

Vector of power values for the current frame:


??? equation "Equations"

    $$Power[k] = \Re(X[k])^2 + \Im(X[k])^2$$

---

### `SpectralPower`

Vector of magnitude values normalized by the FFT size $N$:

??? equation "Equations"

    $$|X[k]| = \frac{\sqrt{Power[k]}}{N}$$

---

### `NormSpectralPower`

Spectral power vector normalized by the sum of all spectral bins:

??? equation "Equations"

    $$|X_{norm}[k]| = \frac{|X[k]|}{\sum_{j=0}^{K-1} |X[j]|}$$

---

### `MFCC`

Mel-frequency cepstral coefficients, using the Discrete Cosine Transform (DCT-II) over mel-band energies $E_m$:

??? equation "Equations"

    $$MFCC[i] = \sum_{m=0}^{M-1} \cos\left( \frac{\pi i (m + 0.5)}{M} \right) \max(L_{min}, 10 \log_{10}(E_m))$$

!!! info "`OpenScofo` `mfcc` descriptors match [`librosa`](https://librosa.org/) implementation with maximum differences on the order of $10^{-4}$."

---

### `Chroma`

Vector of 12 chroma values representing energy distribution across pitch classes $c$, weighted by filter $W$:

??? equation "Equations"

    $$Chroma[c] = \sum_{k=0}^{K-1} W_{c}[k] \cdot Power[k]$$

!!! info "`OpenScofo` `chroma` descriptors match [`librosa`](https://librosa.org/) implementation with maximum differences on the order of $10^{-3}$."

---

### `Silence Probability`

Probability that the current frame corresponds to silence, derived from Loudness ($L$) via a logistic function where $\alpha = 0.25$ and $L_0 = -60.0$:

??? equation "Equations"

    $$P_{silence} = \frac{1}{1 + e^{\alpha (L - L_0)}}$$

---

### `Extended Technique Probability`

Probability that the current frame contains an extended or percussive technique. It uses a raw probability ($p$) inverted from silence, pitch confidence ($C_p$), harmonicity ($H$), and flux ($F$), passed through a sigmoid with steepness $\beta = 25.0$:

??? equation "Equations"

    $$p = (1 - P_{silence}) \cdot (1 - C_p) \cdot (1 - H) \cdot (1 - F)$$

    $$P_{ext} = \frac{1}{1 + e^{-\beta (p - 0.5)}}$$

[^1]: `OpenScofo` uses a Hann window and FFTW3 for FFT.
