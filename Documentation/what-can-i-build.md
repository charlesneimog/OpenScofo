# What Can I Build?

Build live interactions that adapt to the performer in real time.

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

- __Live Processing__

    Trigger delays, freeze sounds, change reverb parameters, and other effects according to the performance.

- __Live Spatialization__

    Create spatial trajectories that follow the performer rather than a fixed timeline. Spatial movement can advance, pause, or change according to the performer's timing.

- __Synthesis__

    Trigger and control Csound or SuperCollider instruments in real time.

- __Media Control__

    Synchronize video, lighting, projections, and other media with the live performance.

</div>
